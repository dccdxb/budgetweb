Everything you need to know before starting your project.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most starter websites ship in 7–10 days. Business & custom projects typically take 2–4 weeks depending on features and content readiness.

What is included in the ₹2,999 launch offer?

A 5-page mobile-first website, a FREE .com or .in domain registered in your name, FREE 1-year premium hosting with SSL, contact form, WhatsApp button and basic SEO setup — for just ₹2,999 (MRP ₹14,999).

Do you provide domain registration and hosting?

Yes. We register domains in your name, set up premium NVMe hosting, free SSL, daily backups and 24×7 monitoring — all managed end-to-end.

What is AI Search Optimization?

It is the practice of structuring your content so AI assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google SGE cite your brand in their answers. We add structured data, authoritative content, FAQs and entity signals to win those placements.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Absolutely. Every site is mobile-first, tested on iPhone and Android across Chrome, Safari, Edge and Samsung Internet at multiple screen sizes.
